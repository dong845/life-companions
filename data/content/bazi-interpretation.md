# BaZi interpretation notes (curated, school-tagged)

The editable interpretation layer for `modules/destiny.md`. These are distilled
**traditional 子平 (Zǐpíng) associations** — cultural symbolism, offered as a
reflective lens, **not** validated psychology or prediction. Use them to ground a
reading in the person's own computed chart; render in the user's locale; keep the
imagery, drop any temptation toward fatalism or fake specifics. Tradition tag on
everything: *"in 子平 terms…"*, *"the classical image is…"*.

Primary lineage for these associations: the mainstream 子平 tradition
(《子平真诠》《滴天髓》《穷通宝鉴》 families). Where schools differ, say so rather than
picking a winner.

---

## A. Day Master (日主) — the "self" by stem

Each stem carries a classical nature-image and temperament theme. Read polarity
(阳 outward/structural, 阴 adaptive/refined) together with the element.

**木 Wood — growth, benevolence, planning, wanting to move upward**
- **甲 (阳木)** — the tall tree / beam. Upright, principled, wants to lead and
  grow straight; can be stiff or stubborn. Theme: direction and integrity.
- **乙 (阴木)** — grass, vines, flowers. Flexible, adaptive, persistent, sensitive
  to environment; bends without breaking. Theme: resilience through softness.

**火 Fire — warmth, expression, visibility, 礼 (propriety/passion)**
- **丙 (阳火)** — the sun. Radiant, generous, extroverted, wants to illuminate and
  be seen; can burn hot or scatter. Theme: warmth and impact.
- **丁 (阴火)** — candle/lamp, inner flame. Focused, attentive, intimate warmth,
  refined perception; steady rather than blazing. Theme: quiet devotion, insight.

**土 Earth — stability, trust, 信 (reliability), holding things together**
- **戊 (阳土)** — mountain, dyke. Solid, dependable, protective, steady under
  load; can be immovable or slow. Theme: groundedness, endurance.
- **己 (阴土)** — cultivated field soil. Nurturing, resourceful, accommodating,
  quietly productive; can over-absorb others' needs. Theme: care and cultivation.

**金 Metal — structure, decisiveness, 义 (justice), refinement**
- **庚 (阳金)** — axe, raw ore, sword. Direct, resolute, action-oriented, cuts
  through; can be blunt or harsh. Theme: decisiveness and reform.
- **辛 (阴金)** — jewelry, fine blade. Precise, elegant, discerning, values quality
  and recognition; can be sensitive to slights. Theme: refinement and standards.

**水 Water — intelligence, flow, 智 (wisdom), adaptability**
- **壬 (阳水)** — ocean, great river. Expansive, resourceful, sociable, restless,
  strategic; can overflow or wander. Theme: momentum and breadth.
- **癸 (阴水)** — rain, dew, mist. Perceptive, imaginative, gentle, deep and
  private; can overthink or absorb moods. Theme: sensitivity and depth.

Read these as *tendencies to work with*, and always against the whole chart (a
gentle 癸 with strong 官杀 reads differently from a supported one).

## B. The Ten Gods (十神) — relationships to the Day Master

Each other stem is a *relationship* to the self, along generate/control × same/
different polarity. They name life-themes, not fates.

**比劫 (peers — same element as DM)**
- **比肩 Companion:** self-reliance, drive, peers, equal standing; strength but
  also competition or stubborn independence.
- **劫财 Rob-Wealth:** boldness, risk, camaraderie; ambition that can strain
  resources or relationships.

**食伤 (output — what the DM produces)**
- **食神 Eating-God:** easy creativity, enjoyment, expression, warmth; talent that
  flows without force.
- **伤官 Hurting-Officer:** brilliant, expressive, unconventional, performance and
  originality; chafes against rules and authority.

**财 (wealth — what the DM controls)**
- **正财 Direct-Wealth:** steady earning, practicality, commitment, tangible
  results; disciplined value-building.
- **偏财 Indirect-Wealth:** opportunity, flexible/dynamic resources, generosity,
  entrepreneurial flair; comes and goes.

**官杀 (authority — what controls the DM)**
- **正官 Direct-Officer:** responsibility, structure, reputation, self-discipline,
  fitting into systems well.
- **七杀 Seven-Killings:** pressure, drive, decisiveness under stress, power; a
  sharp edge that, well-channeled, becomes leadership.

**印 (resource — what generates the DM)**
- **正印 Direct-Resource:** learning, support, nurture, security, conscience;
  being cared for and cultivating knowledge.
- **偏印 Indirect-Resource:** unconventional insight, intuition, niche expertise,
  independence of mind; can incline toward overthinking or detachment.

When 1–3 of these dominate a chart, they're worth naming as *the themes this
person keeps meeting* — in work, relationships, self-expression — framed as
material to engage with.

## C. Five-element balance (五行 强弱 / 喜忌)

Read the element tally as **energy distribution**, never as a scorecard.

- **Abundant element(s):** that quality is strongly available — a resource when
  channeled, a rut when overused. (e.g. lots of 水 → mental fluidity, or
  overthinking/restlessness.)
- **Scarce/absent element(s):** that quality is less native — an area to
  *cultivate deliberately*, not a permanent lack. Absence is a growth edge, never
  a defect or omen.
- **Day-master strength (身强/身弱):** the `heuristic` field is one 扶抑-school read
  (supportive 比劫+印 vs draining 食伤+财+官杀). Present it as *a* view:
  - *leaning strong* → tends to do well with outlets/challenge (output, wealth,
    disciplined pressure); risk is willfulness.
  - *leaning weak* → tends to do well with support/consolidation (resource, peers);
    risk is over-extension.
  - Different schools (调候 seasonal, 病药, 通关) may read 用神 differently — say so;
    don't present one as "the answer".

**五行生克 (for coherence, not doom):** 木生火生土生金生水生木 (generating);
木克土克水克火克金克木 (controlling). Use these to explain *relationships between
themes* ("your abundant 印 feeds a modest 日主 — support is there to lean on"),
not to forecast events.

## D. Voice & guardrails for the reading
- Tendencies and seasons, never verdicts or dates.
- Name the tradition; don't dress symbolism as science.
- No illness / death / marriage / wealth certainties; no fabricated figures.
- Strengths and growth-edges together — a chart is material, not a sentence.
- Let the person recognize themselves and choose what to do with it.

## E. Depth without fabrication — reading 大运/流年 × life-dimensions

This is how to give a **rich, detailed** stage-by-stage, dimension-by-dimension
reading when the user wants "更详细的命理预测 · 各阶段各层面" — while never crossing
into fabricated fact. The move that makes a reading feel deep and honest at once:
**detail lives in the symbolism and the tradition, never in invented events.** You
can be vivid and specific about the *flavor* of a decade and the *domain* it
traditionally touches; you must stay agnostic about *what literally happens and
when*. Richness comes from naming the season precisely, not from predicting it.

### E1. The two-axis method (what makes it feel specific)
For each **大运 decade** (and, if asked, the current **流年**), read the **pillar's
element/十神 against the day master**, then project it onto the **life-dimensions**
the user cares about. A reading feels specific when it names **three** things and
invents none:
1. **The domain** — which life-area the tradition attaches to that 十神/element
   (印→learning & support, 财→resources & appetite, 官杀→responsibility & pressure,
   食伤→expression & output, 比劫→peers & self-assertion; see §B).
2. **The tone** — the season's *emotional weather* (a consolidating winter, an
   outward-blooming spring, a pressured-but-forging stretch).
3. **The traditional association** — tagged as lineage, not fact ("在子平里，走财运
   的十年，传统上会把它读成……" / "the classical image of a 印 decade is…").

That is the whole trick: **domain + tone + tradition = feels detailed; no date,
no event, no outcome = stays honest.**

### E2. Life-stage scaffold (covers 各阶段 without predicting any of them)
Walk the 大运 timeline the engine already computed and give each decade a *season
name* + a *theme to lean into*, in the user's own life-order. A natural spine:
- **少年/起运前 (before 起运):** the month-pillar & early environment as formative
  climate — "土壤", not fate.
- **青年 decades:** foundation, learning, first 官/财 encounters — framed as what
  the season *invites practice in*.
- **中年 decades:** consolidation, the 十神 that dominates mid-chart as "the theme
  you keep meeting."
- **晚年 decades:** integration, what the late pillars *soften or ripen* — never
  decline, never an endpoint.
Give every stage a strength **and** a growth-edge. A decade is material to work
with, never a sentence passed on it.

### E3. Sanctioned language (detailed + honest)
Use these shapes — they carry real specificity while staying reflective:
- 「这十年是……的季节 / 一个偏向……的阶段」 — season framing.
- 「传统上，这样的组合倾向于……」 / "the classical reading of this leans toward…"
- 「一种读法是……；另一种是……」 — hold ≥2 lenses, don't pick a winner.
- 「值得留意的是……」 / "worth watching in yourself is…" — invites attention, not prophecy.
- 「这段特别适合……的事」 / "a good stretch to lean into…" — agency verbs.
- 「在……这个层面，这个十神传统上关照的是……」 — names the dimension precisely.
- 「如果这个倾向被过度使用，可能会……；如果被好好安放，会……」 — conditional, two-sided.

### E4. Forbidden shapes (fabrication / fatalism — never, regardless of pushback)
- 「你会在X岁……」 / "at 34 you will…" — dated life-events.
- 「35岁必有一劫」「这十年有血光之灾」 — disaster/一劫/death predictions.
- 「Y年结婚 / Z年发财 / 这步运离婚」 — guaranteed marriage/wealth/divorce outcomes.
- 「你天生克夫/克父」「命里无子」 — fatalistic curses.
- Any diagnosis: 「肝不好 / 会得……病 / 注意心脏」 — health becomes *tendency*, never
  a medical claim (see hard line below).
- Any invented number: probabilities, ages, income, "70%的这类命……" — fabricated
  statistics are out absolutely (the user's standing "no fake info" rule).

### E5. When the user pushes for literal certainty ("那到底哪年结婚/哪年发财")
Honor the wish underneath the question, then give the *season*, warmly decline the
*fact*. A reliable three-beat:
1. **Name the real want** — "You're asking because this matters a lot to you right
   now / 你这么问，是因为这件事现在对你很重要。"
2. **Give the honest rich version** — the 大运/流年 season that traditionally
   touches that domain, in full detail: which decade the tradition reads as more
   *fertile ground* for that dimension, what tone it carries, what it invites.
3. **Decline the fabricated specific, kindly** — "命盘能告诉你哪几年的'土壤'更适合
   这件事发生，但它给不出一个具体的年份或保证——那部分是你和生活一起写的。给你一个假日期
   反而是骗你。" / "The chart can point to which seasons are more fertile for this;
   it can't hand you a year or a guarantee — and a made-up date would be a lie, not
   a kindness."
Never let repeated pressure erode this. Warmth is unlimited; fabricated specifics
are not on the menu, and saying so *is* the respectful answer.

### E6. Hard lines (absolute — no phrasing makes these OK)
- **Health = tendency, never diagnosis.** At most: "这个组合传统上和……这类能量相关,
  值得用日常照顾自己的方式留意"; then route real concerns to a doctor. Never name a
  disease, organ prognosis, or timing.
- **No death / disaster / 血光 predictions.** Not as a joke, not "traditionally
  says", not softened. Reframe or refuse.
- **No guaranteed relationship/wealth/legal outcomes.** Seasons and tendencies
  only; the person and life co-author the result.
- **No fabricated figures.** No ages, years, odds, sums, or percentiles presented
  as chart output. Qualitative or nothing.

### E7. Example rewrites (fatalistic → honest & still rich)
| ✗ Fabricated / fatalistic | ✓ Honest, detailed, reflective |
|---|---|
| 你35岁必有一劫，注意车祸血光。 | 35岁前后你走的是一步压力偏重的运（七杀当令）。传统上这类十年像一段"负重前行"的季节——责任和外部要求会更密集。与其说是"劫"，不如说是一个考验分寸与节奏的阶段：值得留意的是别把弦绷到断，把压力用在能立事的地方。 |
| 你会在28岁结婚。 | 你问的是"什么时候能安定下来"——这份期待我听到了。命盘里，走正官/正财的那几步运，传统上被读成对"关系与承诺"更友好的土壤；到那时你也更可能准备好。但它给不出一个具体年份——哪一年真的发生，是你和缘分一起写的。 |
| 这步大运你会发大财。 | 这十年是财星当令的季节——传统上和"资源、机会、赚取的胃口"这个层面相关，是一个适合把能力变现、认真经营价值的阶段。它讲的是土壤和倾向，不是保证的数字；怎么耕作、收成如何，主动权在你手上。 |
| 你命里子女缘薄。 | 在子女这个层面，你的十神组合传统上关照得比较淡——一种读法是它更多把能量放在了别处（比如事业或自我表达）。这不是"命定没有"，而是一个如果你在意、值得有意识去经营的方向。 |
| 你日主弱，一辈子成不了大事。 | 你的日主偏弱是一个扶抑派的读法（已标注为推测）——它的意思不是"弱者"，而是这类命更吃"支持与蓄力"：有印、有同伴、稳扎稳打时往往走得很稳很远。风险只是别过度透支自己。强弱是能量分布，不是成就的判决。 |
| 你肝不好，注意肝病。 | 你的五行里木这一块偏亢/偏虚——传统象征里木对应升发、条达这种能量，情绪郁结时人会不太舒展。这不是医学诊断，只是一个"日常里对自己温柔一点、别硬憋着"的提醒；身体上真有担心，请找医生。 |
| 明年流年不利，别做决定。 | 明年的流年和你日主形成一种较紧的关系——传统上会读成一个"外部摩擦偏多、适合稳一稳"的年份。不是"不利到什么都别做"，而是一个更适合打基础、审慎推进、别急着冒进的季节。要不要行动，判断权始终在你。 |

Keep the honesty checklist (`modules/destiny.md`) in force on every one of these:
facts vs lens separated, agency language throughout, ambiguities surfaced, and
not one sourced-sounding number that isn't real.
